# Changelog

## 0.1.0-beta.1 - unreleased - seasons and festivals for MineColonies

What a player gets in this version: a calendar with seasons and real-date festivals, the Decorator (a new
worker and hut who decorates the colony and a tree on the square for the Winter Festival and takes it all
down afterwards), eleven decoration blocks, gifts, a gathering at the tree, New Year fireworks, winter
clothes for colonists, and three effects on the game that are off by default. The player-facing changelog
for the upload is `CHANGELOG-0.1.0-beta.1.md` at the repo root. Below, the notes of each build chunk.

### Build notes

- **S0, skeleton and first boot.** The mod loads next to MineColonies 1.1.1420 on a dedicated server. It
  registers its (still empty) blocks, items, block entities, sounds, MineColonies jobs and buildings, and
  a server config `colony_seasons-server.toml` with every key of the design (calendar, the two festivals'
  dates, the Decorator, the events, the effects, all effects off). Build, probe and check scripts in `tools/`;
  the probes `csboot` and `csconfig` (defaults and seeded) pass.
- **S1, calendar.** The server knows the season and the active festivals, saves them with the world
  (`colony_seasons_calendar`: the offset and a forced festival), tells every client at login and on each change
  (`SeasonStatePayload`), and obeys `/colonyseasons status`, `season set <season>`, `festival force <id>` and
  `festival clear` (permission 2). Festival windows come from the config dates (year turn and 29 Feb handled);
  the season is `(day time / 24000 + offset) mod year length`; a new world starts in `calendar.startSeason`
  (`auto`: the season of the real date). Serene Seasons is followed through a reflection-only bridge, polled every
  20 ticks, with a one-line fall-back to the own calendar. The real clock is read in one class (`DateSource`).
  The chat line to a colony's players when the season turns is not in S1 (it needs colony lookups). Probe `cscalendar`
  (modes dates, bridge-absent, bridge-present, bridge-throws, bridge-nonseasonal, bridge-off), `tools/restart.sh`
  (two server runs on one kept world), a written-here Serene Seasons stand-in for the rig in `tools/sereneshim`.
- **S6, winter clothes.** Colonists and visitors wear a knitted scarf in in-game winter (red, green, blue, mustard or plum, picked by the colonist's id so he keeps it) and a striped festive scarf while the Winter Festival is on; fourteen MineColonies models without headgear of their own (`assets/colony_seasons/clothes/hat_models.json`) also get a wool hat of the scarf's colour in winter, never over a helmet or MineColonies' own December hat. Drawn by one client-only render layer on MineColonies' citizen renderer, nothing saved on the colonist; client config `colony_seasons-client.toml` (`showClothes`, `showParticles`). The season packet now clamps an unknown season id. `tools/clothescheck.sh` (the rules without a game, the clothes data), probe `csclient`, textures from `tools/gen_clothes.py`, previews on MineColonies' models in `docs/renders/s6/` (`tools/preview.sh`).
- **S2, decoration blocks.** Eleven blocks with items, crafting recipes and loot tables, in an own creative tab
  "Colony Seasons": `wreath`, `garland`, `lantern_string`, `fairy_lights` (thin wall blocks: no collision, break at
  once, pop off with the wall behind), `ornament_leaves`, `tinsel_leaves` (never decay), `tree_star`, the two-block
  `large_star`, `gift_pile` (block entity: stock and who took a gift on which real day), `firework_crate` and
  `festival_marker` (block entity: who placed it, its colony). Looks as picked (wreath A, strings A, B and C, trees A
  and B) with larger fairy-light bulbs and baubles; lights have `lit` and shine at full brightness through
  `neoforge_data` elements. Generated by `tools/textures/*.py` and `tools/gen_blockdata.py`; `tools/check_blocks.py`
  in the checks; probe `csblocks`; day and night renders in `docs/renders/s2/`.
- **S3, the Decorator.** A new hut (`colony_seasons:blockhutdecorator`, building `colony_seasons:decorator`, levels 1 to 5,
  one worker, Creativity then Dexterity) and job (`colony_seasons:decorator`) with his own citizen model, skins for both
  genders and four skin tones, borrowed Florist voice lines, a hut block model in the look of the Workshop with
  shopfront, recipe, loot table and lang. All year (setting "prepare all year", on by default) he asks the colony for
  the materials of every decoration kit below the stock cap (8 + 8 x hut level per decoration) and assembles them at
  his hut; kits are datapack files in `data/<namespace>/decorator_kits/`, read by our own loader (a broken kit is
  skipped with one warning); `decorator.needsMaterials = false` makes him ask for and use nothing. The phase of his
  year comes from the calendar; the Decorating, Tending, Taking down, Hosting and Launching states are stubs for S4
  and S7. No hut blueprint yet (S5), so the hut cannot be built with the build tool until then. Probe `csdecorator`
  (three game days with a night and rain on a real colony, mode free) and `tools/restart.sh csdecorator`.
- **S11, page and logo (drafts).** README, the CurseForge page text and one-line summary, the player-facing changelog, a 400x400 logo made by `tools/gen_logo.py` from our own textures (three variants in `docs/renders/s11/`), and `tools/check_public.py` (no personal names, user-folder paths or world names in tracked files, and no personal names or world names, forwards or backwards, in any commit of the history, no machine paths or hype words in public text, the summary at most 120 characters). Sentences that rest on chunks not built yet are listed in `release/VERIFY.md` for the final check after S9.
- **S5, blueprints.** An own Structurize pack "Colony Seasons" (works with every colony style) in the jar: the
  Decorator's hut, look "Workshop with shopfront" (the picked hut A) in five levels on one footprint
  (`decorator/workshop1..5`: a one-storey stone workshop that gains its shopfront, upper floor, awning and roof detail,
  level 5 the picked picture; racks for his stock, a ladder upstairs, lanterns inside, no bed), and the tree pieces the
  Decorator places at the festival marker (`pieces/village_spruce_small`, `_full`, `_rich` from tree A, `grand_tree`
  from tree B; drawn facing north, the marker's cell left as air, tags `gift_pile`, `firework_crate`, `star`, hidden
  from the build tool). Built by `tools/build_pack.py` from `tools/decorator_hut.py` and `tools/tree_pieces.py`; its
  checks (ids and properties, floating and unattached blocks, walking, the picked designs, read-back) are a step of
  `tools/checks.sh`; probe `cspaste` pastes every file through Structurize and reads the world back; renders in
  `docs/renders/s5/`.
- **S8, effects and the hut window.** The three effect switches work and, off, change nothing: `effects.festivalHappiness` (a modifier of our own id on every colonist on festival days, worked out per colonist to add the configured points, put back by the server every 100 ticks because MineColonies drops custom ids on a load, neutral when the festival ends; the total a client is sent equals the server's, the citizen window's list of reasons leaves ours out), `effects.winterCropSlowdown` (about half the growth steps in a claim vetoed in in-game winter through `CropGrowEvent.Pre`, none outside; MineColonies' own crop blocks never fire the event: proven) and `effects.winterFoodNeed` (a quarter of what a colonist used, taken again every 100 ticks in winter). The Decorator's hut window gets a Calendar page (season and day, festival and its dates, his status line as a lang key, decorations up and buildings done, stock against the cap, what the next kit lacks, what the effects do) from an info module and its view (`tools/check_packets.py` compares what the two write and read) and BlockUI layout; his settings gain Decorate and one switch each for wreaths, garlands, lights and the tree, which the planner honours. Probe `cseffects` (modes off and on), `tools/check_packets.py` and `tools/check_windows.py` in the checks.
- **S4, placing, the ledger, taking down.** From the first day of a decoration window the Decorator puts wreaths, garlands and strings of lights on the colony's buildings (town hall first, then the nearest; 2 + 2 x hut level buildings a game day) and the tree at the player's festival marker, tends them once a game day and takes them down after the window, the items back in his hut. Only into air, inside the colony's claim, nothing with collision outside the square, and every block is written to a ledger kept per level outside the hut (`data/colony_seasons_decor.dat`) before the next one goes up; a block comes down only where the ledger lists exactly that state, anything a player or the Builder changed is forgotten and left alone. The wreath goes above the door (two up, else three up) or beside it; doors are found in the building's box by sky light. A building under construction or with an open work order loses its decorations within 100 ticks, a removed building at once (MineColonies' `BuildingRemovedModEvent`), and with no Decorator left the server sweeps them after the window. `/colonyseasons decor list` and `decor remove <colony>` (permission 2). Tree pieces load asynchronously from the pack "Colony Seasons" (from S5); one that does not fit (more than 10 % of its cells blocked, or a trunk cell) is not placed and the managers are told once a day. Also: Creativity sets his pace, "prepare all year" off holds in every phase, a kit larger than the stock cap is made once. Probe `csdecorgate` (real huts of six style packs, edge cases, the square, a real colony with the AI; modes styles, square, lifecycle) and `tools/restart.sh csdecorgate`; `tools/check_decor.py` in the checks.
- **S4b, fix: after a work order the Decorator decorates the building again the same day.** Cause: whether a building had been counted against the day's budget (2 + 2 x hut level buildings a game day) was kept on its ledger group, and taking the decorations off for an upgrade or repair drops that group, so the building needed a second slot of the budget and, with the day's slots used up, stayed bare until the next game day. It showed only after S5: the real tree pieces of the pack then go up on the same game day in the probe's colony and bring the day's count to 10 of 8. Now the colony keeps which buildings and squares it counted that day (saved in the ledger; older files are read too), so the building comes back without a second slot. Also from the S4 review: a visit that plans the same again leaves the ledger as it was and writes no file; a tending visit that is turned away (the building under construction or gone, its chunks not loaded, the tree piece still loading) is not noted as tended, so the group is tended later that day; `decor remove` in a colony with no Decorator's hut says how many items were lost. Probe `csdecorgate`: the work-order check runs with the day's budget used up in every mode, plus a check for each of the three.
- **S7, festival events.** When the Decorator finishes the tree the colony's players are told; a right-click on it by a player of the colony (MineColonies' protection answers first) lights every ornament, the star and the strings on the buildings with a chime (our own, `tools/sfx/chime.py`) and three rockets, and if nobody does within two game days the Decorator lights it; every switch keeps the ledger equal to the world and the lit state is saved (`data/colony_seasons_festival.dat`). The Decorator's gift pile at the tree hands each player one wrapped gift per real day (`events.giftsPerPlayerPerDay`, the claim saved for the whole server), which opens into a roll of `colony_seasons:gifts/winter_daily`, `gifts/winter_festival` on 24 to 26 Dec or `gifts/new_year` (a few festival rockets and a small thing). On the Winter Festival days, in the hours `events.gatherFromTick` to `events.gatherUntilTick` (guarded: never past 10500, never empty), the off-duty colonists walk to the tree (one on MineColonies' own leisure trip elsewhere has it ended), the marker is a MineColonies leisure site, a short waltz plays (`tools/sfx/song.py`) and the Decorator hosts at the tree, handing colonists gifts (hearts and a thank-you line, no item); a soft-fail mixin at the head of `CitizenAI.calculateNextState` brings the working colonists on the first festival game day of each real day (`events.whoComes`, default `everyone_once_a_day`). On the New Year days the Decorator gets the rockets in at the square, and the server launches the show from the firework crate with his stock at the first dusk of each real day with a player in the colony (a sparkle on later dusks), plus a countdown and the big show at real midnight (`events.midnightShow`). One chat line to a colony's players when the season turns (the S1 hand-off). Pulled every second, not every 5 s as design 5 suggests (MineColonies' idle walk wanders off between pulls). Probe `csfestival` (the brief's name for the plan's `festivalrun`; modes nomixin and voyager: Voyager 0.4.0's night-shift mixin and ours apply side by side) and `tools/restart.sh csfestival`; `tools/check_festival.py` in the checks; the gift sheet and the two sounds in `docs/renders/s7/`.
