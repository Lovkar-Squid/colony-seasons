# Colony Seasons - Seasons and Festivals for MineColonies

**Discord:** [discord.gg/BVztrTtXFu](https://discord.gg/BVztrTtXFu) - questions, dev builds and bug reports.

**Coming soon.** The first version is planned for early December 2026, in time for the Winter Festival. This
page is here so you can follow it. Below is what the first version, 0.1.0, will have.

Colony Seasons gives your colony a calendar. The game has seasons, and the festivals come on real dates. In
December a new worker, the Decorator, hangs wreaths, garlands and lights on the colony's buildings and builds
a decorated tree on the square. Colonists put on scarves, they gather at the tree on the festival days, you
pick up a small gift every day, and New Year ends with fireworks. After 6 January the Decorator takes it all
down again.

## The Decorator

A new MineColonies job with his own building, the Decorator's Workshop, levels 1 to 5. All year he asks the
warehouse for leaves, string, dye, paper and glowstone dust and makes decorations, so they are ready when
December comes. Then he walks from building to building and puts up what his level allows: a wreath on the
main door at level 1, garland and lights beside it at higher levels, and a bigger tree on the square, up to a
grand tiered tree at level 5.

He only places blocks into empty air inside his own colony, and he writes down every block he places. When
the festival is over he takes down exactly those blocks. Anything you built yourself is left alone.

## The tree, the gifts and the fireworks

You choose the square with a Festival Marker, and the tree goes there. You light it with a right-click. The
pile at its foot holds one small gift per player per day, a better one on 24 to 26 December, when the
colonists gather at the tree in the afternoon. On 31 December and 1 January there are fireworks at dusk and
a show at midnight if the server runs through it.

## Seasons and clothes

The game year is 48 game days by default, twelve to a season. If Serene Seasons is installed, Colony Seasons
follows its calendar. In winter colonists wear a knitted scarf, and a wool hat if their model has no headgear
of its own. It is all drawn on the client; nothing goes into their inventory.

## Effects on the game

Three switches, all off by default: a happiness bonus on festival days, slower crop growth in winter and more
food eaten in winter.

## Requirements

NeoForge 1.21.1 and MineColonies 1.1 (1.1.1420 or newer) with Structurize and Domum Ornamentum. Serene Seasons
is optional. Only the Winter Festival and New Year are in the first version; the other festivals come later,
each before its real date.
